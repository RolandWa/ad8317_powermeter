# ADL5507 against AD8317 as the RF detector of the power meter

Scope: compare the ADL5507 (Analog Devices, 10 MHz - 12 GHz log detector, in `doc/adl5507.pdf`) with
the AD8317 that the board uses, and simulate the S11 at the SMA connector J1 with each of them
behind the same input lines. Everything here is a circuit model (Qucs), not a field solution and
not a measurement; read it with the limits in the last section.

Files (all in `sim/qucs/`):

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `adl5507_input_model.py` | ADL5507 RFIN model; writes the S-parameter files |
| `ADL5507_RFIN_1MHz_10GHz_raw.s1p` | bare RFIN pin, datasheet Table 5, 401 log points 1 MHz - 10 GHz |
| `ADL5507_RFIN_1MHz_10GHz_shunt51.s1p` | RFIN with the 51 ohm shunt, datasheet Table 4 |
| `run_adl5507_study.py` | builds and runs the cases, writes the CSV, the plots and the tables below |
| `case_E*.net`, `case_E*.sch` | qucsator netlists and Qucs 0.0.19 schematics (`make_qucs_schematics.py`; `.sch` files are git-ignored, they hold absolute paths) |
| `adl5507_vs_ad8317.csv`, `adl5507_vs_ad8317_chips.png`, `adl5507_vs_ad8317_board.png` | results |

`verify_qucs_schematics.py` checks that Qucs turns every schematic into a netlist with the same S11
as the qucsator netlist (all pass, old and new cases).

## 1. Datasheet comparison

| Item | AD8317 (Rev. D) | ADL5507 (Rev. 0) |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency range | 1 MHz - 10 GHz (accurate log conformance to 8 GHz) | 10 MHz - 12 GHz |
| RF input | differential pair INHI/INLO, DC common mode VPOS - 0.6 V | one pin RFIN to COMM, internal 25 pF series capacitor (corner about 4 MHz) |
| +/-1 dB dynamic range (25 C) | 50 dB at 0.9, 1.9, 2.2, 5.8 GHz; 42 dB at 3.6 GHz; 44 dB at 8 GHz | 51.7 dB at 10 MHz, 54.9 dB at 2.4 GHz, 55.0 dB at 3.6 GHz, 55.3 dB at 5.8 GHz, 51.5 dB at 8 GHz, 50.9 dB at 10 GHz |
| Input range for +/-1 dB | about -53 dBm to -3 dBm (0.9 GHz) | -56.2 dBm to -1.2 dBm (3.6 GHz) |
| Slope | -22 mV/dB (decreasing output) | +18.4 ... 19.0 mV/dB in positive mode; negative mode selected with ENBL = VPOS/2 |
| Intercept | +11 ... +21 dBm | -60.9 ... -66.0 dBm (different definition: output offset at the bottom of the range) |
| Supply | 3.0 - 5.5 V, 22 mA typical | 2.7 - 3.45 V, 12.5 mA |
| Disabled current | 200 uA | < 100 uA |
| Output response | 6 ns fall / 10 ns rise (CLPF open), 18 / 20 ns with 8 pF; video bandwidth 50 MHz | 200 ns rise and fall in positive mode, 300 ns in negative mode; output noise 270 nV/sqrt(Hz) at 100 kHz (positive mode) |
| Modes | measurement and controller (VSET) | measurement only (positive or negative slope) |
| Temperature range | -40 ... +85 C | -40 ... +105 C |
| Package | 2 x 3 mm LFCSP-8 | 0.8 x 1.2 mm 6-ball WLCSP |
| Specified input match | 52.3 ohm at INHI (test circuit) | 51 ohm from RFIN to ground |

What this means for the power meter:

* The ADL5507 gives 2 GHz more at the top and a flatter, slightly larger +/-1 dB range, with half
  the current and a much smaller package.
* It loses the range 1 - 10 MHz (the AD8317 is specified from 1 MHz), is 20 - 30 times slower, and has
  no controller mode. At 8 - 10 GHz its +/-1 dB range drops to about 51 dB; the AD8317 drops to
  44 dB at 8 GHz.
* The slope is 18.7 against 22 mV/dB: the voltage step per dB is 15 % smaller, so the ADC
  resolution per dB is 15 % worse. In positive mode the output rises with the input, so the
  conversion from voltage to power in the firmware changes its sign and its constants; the
  calibration procedure stays the same.
* The input pad of the ADL5507 is one pad. INLO, R2, C2 and the C2 return path are not needed.

## 2. Input model

AD8317: `ad8317_input_model.py` (datasheet Table 1 as R || C at 0.9 ... 8 GHz, Figure 15 above
that), INHI - INLO as a series 2-port (the two-pad structure of the board). See
`S11_Analysis_J1_to_AD8317.md` for its limits: at 5.8 GHz the table and the chart disagree.

ADL5507: the datasheet gives S11 (real, imaginary) at 18 points, 10 MHz - 15 GHz, for the bare pin
(Table 5) and with the 51 ohm resistor (Table 4). The tables were read with `pdftotext`; each point was
checked against the impedance printed beside it (all within 0.01). Between the points S11 is interpolated
(real and imaginary part, linear in log f). Below 10 MHz, where the datasheet has no data, the bare
input is extended as a constant resistance (1025 ohm) with a capacitive reactance that follows 1/f.
The Table 4 variant is the extension in parallel with 51 ohm, shifted to meet the table at 10 MHz.

Check of the two tables with each other: the bare input in parallel with an ideal 51 ohm gives
Table 4 within 0.004 (0.01 GHz) to 0.03 (2 GHz) in S11; above 3 GHz Table 4 differs by 0.06 - 0.18,
because ADI measured it with a real resistor on their board (its parasitics are in the table). The
simulation shows both: the ideal 51 ohm (cases E0i, E2) and Table 4 (E0s, E3).

## 3. Cases

| Case | Circuit |
|---|---|
| A0r, A0t | AD8317 INHI to ground, bare / with 52.3 ohm. Chip alone at a 50 ohm port |
| E0r, E0s, E0i | ADL5507 bare (Table 5) / Table 4 / bare + ideal 51 ohm. Chip alone |
| C | AD8317 behind the board lines (`run_s11_study.py`, cases C1 - C4: SUBST + CLIN/CSTEP or MLIN/MSTEP, real geometry of J1 to U1) |
| E1 | the same board, the ADL5507 at the place of the AD8317: R1 = R2 = 100 ohm shunts, C1 47 nF, INLO side not used ("drop-in") |
| E2 | the same lines, no R1/R2/C1, one 51 ohm (0.25 nH ESL) at the pin: the reference circuit of ADI |
| E3 | the same lines as E2, the chip from Table 4 (51 ohm included) |

Four stackups, as in the AD8317 study: coplanar over B.Cu 1.44 mm (as built: In1/In2 are void under
the RF path), microstrip over B.Cu, and the what-if cases with a solid In1 plane 0.1 mm below.

## 4. Results

### 4.1 The chips alone (50 ohm reference)

| Input | RL >= 10 dB up to | RL >= 15 dB up to | |S11| at 3.6 GHz | at 5.8 GHz | at 10 GHz |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AD8317 bare | - | - | -2.6 dB | -8.3 dB | -1.1 dB |
| AD8317 + 52.3 ohm | 6.3 GHz | 3.5 GHz | -14.8 dB | -15.2 dB | -6.0 dB |
| ADL5507 bare | - | - | -1.3 dB | -1.6 dB | -2.3 dB |
| ADL5507 + 51 ohm, Table 4 | 4.9 GHz | 3.0 GHz | -12.9 dB | -8.5 dB | -2.5 dB |
| ADL5507 + ideal 51 ohm | 5.9 GHz | 3.0 GHz | -13.6 dB | -10.1 dB | -3.6 dB |

Both bare inputs reflect nearly everything (mismatch loss 6.7 dB for the AD8317 and 7.4 dB for the
ADL5507, mean over 10 MHz - 6 GHz), so both need the external 50 ohm termination; with it the loss
is 0.02 - 0.05 dB. The AD8317 keeps a useful match to a higher frequency (6.3 GHz against 4.9 GHz for the
datasheet data of the ADL5507). The AD8317 values at and above 5.8 GHz carry the uncertainty of its
table/chart disagreement; the ADL5507 values are the published measurements.

## 5. Conclusions

1. The chip is not what limits S11 at J1 above 3 GHz: on the as-built board the AD8317 and the
   ADL5507 with the same R1/R2 front end (E1) both reach RL = 10 dB at about 3.3 GHz. The lines, the
   launch and the shunt stubs decide. The simulation of the AD8317 study says the same.
2. With the ADL5507 the front end becomes simpler and better when it follows ADI: one 51 ohm
   resistor at the pin (E2, E3) gives RL >= 10 dB to 4.5 - 5.1 GHz on the as-built lines, 1.2 - 1.8 GHz
   more than the drop-in with R1/R2 (E1). Above 5 GHz the ADL5507 input falls to about 8 - 15 ohm
   resistive (datasheet Table 5), and S11 reaches -2 ... -8 dB; the AD8317 model is better there, but it is the
   less certain model.
3. Below 10 MHz the ADL5507 does not work (internal 25 pF, corner 4 MHz): the AD8317 is the one for the
   range from 1 MHz.
4. For the power-meter requirements of `RF_Input_Targets_vs_Lab_Power_Meters.md` (RL >= 15 dB, goal 20 dB
   for a lab-grade meter) neither chip reaches the goal above about 3 GHz on this board. Up to 2 GHz both
   do (RL 17 - 21 dB at 1.9 GHz, 24 - 28 dB at 0.9 GHz).
5. Recommendation: keep the AD8317 for the 1 MHz - 10 GHz meter. The ADL5507 is a good candidate for a
   second, smaller board for 10 MHz - 6 GHz (lower power, one input pad) if the 200 - 300 ns response
   is enough; for it use the 51 ohm at the pin, put it as close to the ball as the layout allows, and
   take the 0.8 x 1.2 mm WLCSP land pattern from the datasheet (the 0.127 mm minimum feature of the
   current design rules is not checked for it here).

## 6. Limits

* A circuit model of the board: no connector launch, no pad-to-trace step capacitance, no radiation,
  ESL of R/C parts is a fixed 0.25 nH. The field solution of the same board (rfsim) did not agree with the
  circuit model yet (see `S11_Analysis_J1_to_AD8317.md`); a VNA measurement is the reference.
* ADL5507 Table 4 and Table 5 are published values at 18 frequencies; the interpolation between them
  (0.5 - 1 GHz steps above 1 GHz) can hide a narrow resonance, and the values below 10 MHz are extrapolated.
* The AD8317 model above 5.8 GHz is uncertain (table and chart of the datasheet differ).
* Chip-to-chip spread: ADI quotes about +/-20 % for RIN and CIN of the ADL5507; the 51 ohm resistor
  dominates, so the S11 spread follows the resistor tolerance (a 1 % resistor keeps it small).
